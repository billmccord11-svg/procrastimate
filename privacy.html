ProcrastiMate Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

The short version: ProcrastiMate keeps everything on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not collect, sell or share your information.

What the app stores

Your school's bell schedule, your class names, the assignments and events you add, and your reminder settings are saved only on your own phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

What we collect

Nothing. ProcrastiMate has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. We do not receive your homework, your schedule, your location or any information about you.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. No server is involved, and nothing about your reminders leaves your phone.

Microphone and speech

If you tap the microphone button, ProcrastiMate asks your phone to turn what you say into text so it can fill in an assignment. When your phone supports it, this happens right on your phone. On some phones, the speech is processed by your phone's own speech service (Apple or Google), under their privacy policies. ProcrastiMate does not record, keep or send your voice. The microphone is only used while you are using the mic button.

Paste and share

The app reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste from a message. It sends a list of what's due only when you tap Send what's due to a parent and choose who to send it to, using your phone's own sharing menu.

Students and children

ProcrastiMate is made for students. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
